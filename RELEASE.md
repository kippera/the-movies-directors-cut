## The Movies: Directors Cut v1.0

A sharper movie viewer, real export sizes, MP4 export, widescreen UI touches and colour settings for The Movies: Stunts & Effects. Works out of the box on GE-Proton 10-33 with no winetricks. The Windows build is experimental.

### Downloads
* **TheMoviesDirectorsCut-Linux-Proton.zip**: everything, tested on GE-Proton 10-33 with DXVK at 2560x1440.
* **TheMoviesDirectorsCut-Windows-experimental.zip**: graphics proxy, export presets and widescreen UI files only.

### Highlights
* Movie viewer renders at 1024 or 2048 px (was 512).
* Export presets produce real 428x240 to 1920x1080 files.
* Linux: MP4 (H.264 + AAC) export, and an export that no longer freezes at "Saving Sound Data".
* Colour grading, FXAA, sharpen and MSAA with an in-game panel (Ctrl+Shift+F7).
* Round HUD buttons stay round at 16:9; clipped text tails repaired.
